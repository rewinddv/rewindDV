MediaInfoLib metadata field references and adapted mappings
---------------------------------------------------------
Selected IEC consumer-DV camera, user-bit, recording-clock and caption-payload
layouts/mappings are adapted into independent, bounded Swift decoders from:
https://github.com/MediaArea/MediaInfoLib
Pinned commit: 6ce87668473b0591c323a66842c937aa1cf76bdf
Source: Source/MediaInfo/Multiple/File_DvDif.cpp and File_DvDif_Analysis.cpp.
No MediaInfo library, DVRescue merge engine or third-party capture backend is
linked. Rewind Digital added application/section/transmission guards,
conflict preservation, strict BCD validation and source-bound reporting.

DVRescue nominal DV25 macroblock geometry and interoperability fixtures
---------------------------------------

commit 5cead7a5dae4ec7ffdf24115c8e3bc6d9c05c033 into bounded native Swift.

many_attributes.dv.dvrescue.xml fixture from the same pinned revision.
